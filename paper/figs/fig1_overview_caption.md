**A cell is a distribution, and a decision language model decodes it.** (1) A cell's UMI count vector is normalized into a probability vector on the gene simplex (alternatives: smoothed, log1p, square-root). (2) A language model with a softmax decision head reads a text state, either a cell type (conditional generation) or a perturbation together with a control cell (perturbation response), and outputs an exactly normalized distribution $q$; training matches $q$ to the distribution of a real cell by soft cross-entropy (dashed). (3) Counts of a generated cell are drawn from a multinomial with a library size. (4) Generated cells are scored against held-out real cells of unseen donors or perturbations. Bar heights are schematic, not data.
