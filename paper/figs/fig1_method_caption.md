**Why generate a sentence?** The same prompt, answered two ways. Top: a cell-sentence model writes the cell as gene names ranked by expression, token by token, and a heuristic map turns the ranks back into expression. Bottom: a decision head answers in one forward pass with one probability for every gene of a fixed, ordered panel; that distribution is the cell, can be sampled, and is scored by an exact likelihood. Bars: the distribution decoded for the test perturbation FOXA1+FOXL2 on 20 of the 2048 panel genes.
