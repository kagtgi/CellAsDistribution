**Detailed overview of the decoder, its training target and its evaluation.** (1) The state is a text prompt: a cell type, or a perturbation with a randomly paired control cell serialized as its 48 most abundant genes with quantized log-mass levels. (2) A LoRA-tuned Qwen3.5-0.8B encodes the prompt; a linear decision head with an optional fixed log-prior $b$ gives gene logits and a softmax gives the exactly normalized distribution $q$. (3) Training matches $q$ to the transformed distribution $T(x)$ of a real cell by soft cross-entropy. (4) A shared sampler (multinomial or Dirichlet-multinomial with a training-fit concentration) draws a generated cell, scored against held-out real cells by NLL, MMD, classifier AUC, gene-mean correlation, cell-type recovery and UMAP.
